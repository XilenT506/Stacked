# STACKED asset library

Made with Higgsfield during the free period (November 2026). Everything below is ours to use in the game, on Steam and in marketing.

- 3D models already in the game: `assets/people/` (originals) and `assets/people/small/` (shrunk for the game, `tools/shrink_glb.sh`).
- Hero model and its back view: `assets/hero/`. Steam capsules cut to size: `assets/store/`.
- Voice clips already cut and in the game: `assets/voices/` (index: `js/voicefiles.gen.js`, made by `tools/gen_voices.mjs`).
