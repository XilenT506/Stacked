# Round 3 critic: feel and comedy of the four errands

Screenshots are in this folder. Times are game seconds, and the browser ran slowly while these were taken.

## Funny in every errand?
| Errand | Funny? | Best moment |
|---|---|---|
| Shop | Yes | Mabel: "Are you lost, sir?" The head nods. "Lost? You said you were lost?" |
| Bank | Partly | Three kids in a row in front of Ruth, with the coat torn open |
| Cinema | Yes | A tiptoe fall in front of Pip: the coat flat on the pavement, popcorn on the ground, Pip's mouth a round O |
| Drive | Yes | A kid's face peeking out of the coat's belly in a tiny L-plate car, and swerving through the cones |

## What fell flat, and what was done
| Finding | Fix (done) |
|---|---|
| Speech bubbles overlap, or sit under the top bar | Each person shows one bubble at a time, and bubbles are drawn lower |
| A fall turns into BUSTED within 0.6–2.4 s, so you never see "TIMBER!" | One fall can now raise the "?" by at most 0.35 in the shop and 0.55 elsewhere |
| The heavy cash or snacks knock the stack over within about 1 s of grabbing them | The weight now builds up over 1.5 s after the grab |
| The height check is only "hold tiptoe for 2 s", so it's the safest moment of the errand | Tiptoe now sways more with snacks in hand. Pip doubts you partway through and if you stand there without stretching. The check now takes 2.5 s |
| The height sign is too high to see | It's lowered and placed next to Pip |
| Officer Hale is silent in the cinema and the console shows errors (audio.js) | Each person's voice pitch now comes from a list of 7, so any number of people work |
| After the stop line, the examiner says "Now park in the box" | He now says "Very good, sir." The park line plays when the park step starts |
| In the car, the on-screen controls still show walking and grabbing | There are car controls for each part: gas and brake, the wheel, mirrors and nod. Price's state is shown too |
| The "Bluffs count double" banner uses a word the test never explains | It now says "He's not looking! Nods and hat tips count double." |
| Bank: the intro text covers the middle of the screen | Not changed. It's a 4.5 s note at the start of each errand |
| A bust never shows a shocked face | Not changed. Moved to round 4 (a "bust pop" moment) |

## Not tested
Online play, sound (the browser ran headless), a whole errand played by hand, and the STOP shout on camera.
